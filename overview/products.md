## RISE with SAP on IBM {{site.data.keyword.powerSys_notm}}
{: #rise-with-sap-powervs}

RISE with SAP on {{site.data.keyword.powerSysFull}} is an enterprise grade private cloud offering for customers who want to modernize their SAP landscape while preserving the proven reliability, security, and performance of IBM Power. It combines SAP&reg; Cloud ERP Private with IBM {{site.data.keyword.powerSys_notm}} to provide a low risk, outcome driven journey to RISE- especially for organisations running long standing, mission critical SAP workloads on IBM Power.

Using IBM {{site.data.keyword.powerSys_notm}} you can rapidly transform on-premises SAP ERP systems, modernize business processes, and become more agile. Known for its high security, scalability and reliability, IBM Power servers are engineered for fewer disruptions and facilitates faster migration, supported by the highly resilient and secured {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} platform.

IBM {{site.data.keyword.powerSys_notm}} is the cloud-based extension of the IBM Power platform, which has hosted mission critical SAP systems for 30+ years. It is engineered for enterprise workloads that require **exceptional availability, stability, and security**, and it is recognized as **ranked #1 in availability among SAP certified server platforms according to [ITIC’s 2024 Global Server Hardware, Server OS Reliability Survey](https://techchannel.com/industry-news/server-reliability-survey-2024/){: external}**. The platform is designed to reduce operational risk, minimize unplanned downtime, and support stringent enterprise KPIs.

Running on the highly resilient and secure {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} infrastructure, IBM {{site.data.keyword.powerSys_notm}} enables a faster and non disruptive transition from on premises SAP systems to RISE with SAP. For customers already running SAP on IBM Power, the offering supports **up to 25% faster migration to SAP Cloud ERP Private**, helping avoid re platforming complexity and reduce overall transformation risk.

As part of RISE with SAP, **SAP Cloud ERP Private (SAP S/4HANA Cloud, private edition)** is delivered as a fully managed service by SAP. SAP is responsible for infrastructure operations, technical managed services, lifecycle management, and service delivery in alignment with the agreed service level agreements (SLAs). This operating model supports defined enterprise KPIs for system availability, incident handling, change management, and recoverability.

The solution is provided as a **single tenant managed private environment**. For each customer, SAP creates a **separate {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} account**. All SAP application and database virtual server instances are **solely dedicated to a single client**, ensuring strong isolation, predictable performance, and support for compliance and data sovereignty requirements.

**Key Benefits**:
- **Enterprise-grade reliability on IBM Power**: Built for mission critical SAP workloads with fewer disruptions and strong platform stability.
- **Low risk SAP modernization**: Particularly suited for customers running SAP on IBM Power on premises, enabling continuity during the transition to RISE with SAP.
- **Single tenant private cloud model**: Dedicated environment with full isolation of application and database resources.
- **SAP-managed operations with clear KPIs**: Reduced operational burden with SAP accountable for service delivery and defined SLA-based outcomes.
- **Secure-by-design architecture**: End to end security across platform, infrastructure, and operations.

## RISE with SAP – Premium Supplier
{: #premium-supplier}

Premium Suppliers are SAP partners who have demonstrated expertise in RISE with SAP and offer a wide range of services. The IBM RISE with SAP – Premium Supplier is a service offered by IBM Consulting that:
- Delivers Premium Supplier Services to SAP under the same service level agreement as Standard RISE.
- Uses the IBM’s HA clustered solution allowing for 99.9% SLA around system availability under the extended service level agreement option from SAP.
- Are built on the same Technical Architecture as Standard RISE.
- IBM’s Well Architected Digital Operations Management Plane that is built on IBM’s own Cloud Tenant, giving clients more control.
- Enables better total cost of ownership (TCO) efficiency with incremental scalability.

## IBM ManagePlus
{: #sap-offerings-and-products-ibm-manageplus}

IBM ManagePlus is a service offered by IBM Consulting and can be layered onto RISE with SAP or RISE with SAP – Premium Supplier, where it offers:

* An assurance service which reduces the risk in delivering implementation services.
* Built on the RISE RACI roles and responsibilities providing complete transparency.
* Provides a wrapper service complimenting the RISE Standard Services.
* Based on pre-packaged options, tailored packages also available based on client’s custom requirements.
* Integrated into the RISE Adoption Framework.



IBM ManagePlus can cover non-RISE SAP and non-SAP services based on the client requirements to deliver a seamless, comprehensive and end-to-end service.


IBM ManagePlus increases client value, due to the following:

* Multi-cloud / hybrid cloud orchestration.
* IBM built control plane that allows organizations to adopt IT operational management best practices based on AI and automation.
* Ability to address customer unique requirements such as tailored security standards.
